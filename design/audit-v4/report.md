# LitRun Visual Audit

## 1. Selected Compact Draft

Health: Good structure, still visually oversized.

- The five-metric hierarchy is clear and the two control rows are easy to scan.
- The wordmark, telemetry values, gear, and row symbols compete at similar visual weight.
- The active state uses too much green; the status dot alone is enough.
- The fan slider begins too far from its title block, leaving an avoidable gap.
- The mock does not prove keyboard focus, contrast in dark mode, switch hit targets, or Dynamic Type behavior.

## 2. Polished Target

Health: Ready to use as the implementation target.

- Reduced the wordmark and made the status text neutral.
- Tightened telemetry type and unit hierarchy while preserving readability.
- Matched symbol weights and reduced separator emphasis.
- Pulled the fan slider closer to its label and retained only normal bottom padding.
- Implementation still needs light/dark, Chinese/English, Reduce Motion, keyboard, VoiceOver, and small-display verification.
